VQEAF OS v2.1: signed QEAPP/2 Inbox.

Only .qeapp packages signed by the public key compiled into the firmware
can be installed. Unsigned QEAPP/1 and foreign publisher signatures fail.
The two sample apps were signed by the firmware's demo public key.

Create YOUR key pair and sign YOUR packages on your PC; keep your
private PEM key secret and never copy it to microSD or share it.
See docs/QEAPP_V15_SIGNING.md.
