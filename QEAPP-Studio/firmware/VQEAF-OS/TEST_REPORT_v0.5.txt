Symbian S3 OS v0.5 - System Services Edition
Date: 2026-09-23
Target: ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 N16R8, ST7789 portrait 240x320

[Implemented]
- Quick panel: WiFi radio, backlight, volume, Notifications, Lock.
- Notification center: bounded 12-event in-RAM queue, unread/read state, delete/clear.
- Keypad lock: START/SELECT unlock, 10-second lock-screen backlight dimming.
- Auto-lock setting: Off / 30 s / 60 s / 2 min / 5 min.
- Persistent Notes app backed by Preferences/NVS and existing on-screen keyboard.
- Idle/Home unread notification + background music state.
- New shortcuts: Idle OPTION Quick panel, Idle UP Notifications, A/B Lock, hold A Notifications, hold B Lock.
- Settings list vertical scrolling for 7 items.
- Fixed duplicate NimBLEScan scanner declaration.
